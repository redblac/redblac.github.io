## 🛠️ How to Use Masthead

### Your First Launch
When you open masthead for the first time, you'll see a welcome screen asking you to select your country. Pick yours, and masthead instantly builds your personalized front page.

### Reading Articles
Simply click any headline to open the full article in a clean, readable format. All images, captions, and quotes are preserved, but the ads, pop-ups, and tracking scripts are gone.

### Changing Your Country
Go to **Settings** → **Country** to switch between any of the six supported countries. Your front page updates immediately with that country's news sources.

### Sorting & Filtering
You can sort headlines by **Latest** or **Most Read**, and filter by category using the navigation bar at the top. Want only tech news? One click.

### Bookmarking
Found something important? Click the bookmark icon to save it for later. Your saved articles are stored locally on your device—never in the cloud.

### Keyboard Shortcuts
For power readers:
- `N` – Next article
- `P` – Previous article
- `B` – Bookmark current article
- `D` – Toggle dark mode
- `R` – Refresh news feed

## 🧭 Troubleshooting

### ❗ The app won't download
Try a different browser, or right-click the download link and select "Save link as..." This usually resolves download issues.

### ❗ The app opens but shows no news
Check your internet connection. Then click the refresh icon in the top right corner. If the problem persists, close and reopen the app.

### ❗ I see an "unknown publisher" warning on Windows
This is normal for open-source software. Click **"More info"** → **"Run anyway"**. This security prompt appears because masthead isn't signed with a commercial certificate, but it's safe to run.

### ❗ The app looks tiny on my high-resolution screen
Go to Settings → Display and increase the zoom level. Masthead scales beautifully on 4K screens.

### ❗ I found a bug or have a suggestion
Visit the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/redblac/masthead) and open an issue. The team responds quickly.
